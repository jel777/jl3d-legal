Common questions

How do I get a church access key?

Ask your church's Planning Center Services administrator. Your administrator creates a dedicated viewer-only Planning Center person, generates a Personal Access Token (an Application ID and a Secret) for that person, and shares it privately with band members. The key is read-only and can be revoked by your administrator at any time.

What happens if our church access key is revoked?

Automatic and manual updates pause and the app tells you Planning Center access was removed. Your already-downloaded songs stay readable offline on your device so you are never stuck during a service. Ask your church administrator for a new key, or sign out in Settings.

How do I remove the songs and access key from this device?

Open Settings, tap Sign Out, and choose Sign Out and Remove Downloaded Songs (or Sign Out and Remove Songs on iOS). That deletes the downloaded library, saved lists, recent searches, and saved access key from your device without changing anything in Planning Center. If you only want to stop updates while keeping the downloaded songs offline, choose Sign Out and Keep Songs on This Device. Uninstalling the app also deletes all app data.

Search shows old lyrics or an older chart.

Open the app while online and let it refresh, or go to Settings > Verify all charts now before rehearsal or a service.

Is FDJ Song Finder affiliated with Planning Center?

No. FDJ Song Finder is an independent app made by JL3D Services, LLC. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Planning Center or Ministry Centered Technologies.

The app says the song library cannot be opened.

Nothing has been deleted automatically. Close and reopen the app. If the message stays, you can tap Delete it and start over (Android) to clear the damaged file and re-sync, or email JL3D@proton.me before deleting the app.
