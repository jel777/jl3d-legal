Common questions

I can't hear anything.

Turn the volume up with the side buttons. The violin plays through the media channel and is meant to be heard, so it also plays when the silent switch is on. Check that no Bluetooth device has taken the audio.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone or iPad?

Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen or in Settings while signed in with the same Apple Account.

I reinstalled and my people and history are gone.

Deleting the app removes the people and play history stored on the device. Use Restore Purchases to recover an eligible Pro purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund from Apple.
