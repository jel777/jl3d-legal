Garage Log Support

Keep every vehicle's service history, reminders and recalls in one place.

Contact us: JL3D@proton.me. Include your device model, iOS version and, if you can, the app version (iPhone Settings › Apps › the app name). We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

How do I back up my garage or move to a new iPhone?

In Settings, tap Back Up Garage, then Save to Files (for example to iCloud Drive) or Share or AirDrop. The file contains all vehicles, records, reminders and photos. It is not encrypted, so keep it private. On the new device, tap Restore from Backup in Settings, or tap the file in the Files app and choose Garage Log.

What happens when I restore a backup?

Garage Log checks the file and shows what it contains compared with what is on the device. Nothing changes until you tap Replace My Garage and confirm. Restoring replaces everything on that device, so you can back up the current garage first from the same screen. If anything goes wrong, your current garage is put back automatically.

Can I open my records in a spreadsheet?

Yes. In Settings, tap Export Service History to make a CSV file for Numbers, Excel or Google Sheets. It does not include photos and cannot be restored into the app; use Back Up Garage for that.

My reminders don't appear.

Check that Service Reminders is on in the app's Settings and that notifications are allowed for Garage Log in the iOS Settings app; the app shows a banner when they are turned off. Mileage reminders use the odometer reading you enter, so update it from time to time; the app can remind you to do that.

Recalls don't load.

New recall and complaint lookups need an internet connection. Results already fetched stay available offline with a Last updated date.

Does my car have an open recall?

The recall list in the app is matched by year, make and model, not your VIN. For a vehicle saved with a VIN, tap Check open recalls for this VIN under NHTSA Recalls. It opens NHTSA's lookup on NHTSA.gov, which lists recalls not yet repaired on that car (last 15 years, from the manufacturer). The VIN is also copied, so paste it if the page doesn't fill it in. Your dealer can confirm recall work already completed.

Can I use kilometres?

Yes. In Settings, choose Distance Units: Miles, Kilometers, or Automatic to follow your region.

The app says my garage couldn't be opened, or a restore didn't finish.

Don't delete the app: that would delete your data and its safety copy. Use Save a Copy on that screen, free up some storage, tap Try Again, and email us if it still doesn't open.
