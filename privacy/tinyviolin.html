World's Tiniest Violin Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · JL3D Services, LLC

In short: People, play history and preferences are stored on your iPhone or iPad. Apple handles the optional Pro purchase.

What the app stores on your device

  • The names or nicknames you add for people you play the violin for, plus their saved size and sound preferences.
  • Your play history (up to the most recent 100 plays) and simple statistics derived from it.
  • App preferences such as haptics, shuffle and your selected sound and size.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device. iOS may include it in your own iCloud or computer backup; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What leaves your device

Apple App Store (purchases)
Apple processes the optional Pro purchase through StoreKit 2. JL3D receives no payment card, name or contact details from Apple for the purchase. The app verifies Apple-signed transactions on your device to check Pro access.

JL3D runs no servers for this app and receives none of this data.

Permissions

The app asks for no special permissions.

Purchases

The app is free to download and play. Pro is an optional one-time purchase priced at US$0.99 in the U.S. App Store. Your local price is shown in the app. Pro unlocks two Drama phrases, Grand Lament and Sob Story. Existing eligible purchases from earlier versions continue to unlock Pro.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in with JL3D.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no use of the advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page is updated before the new version is released, and the effective date above changes.
