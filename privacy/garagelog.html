Garage Log Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · JL3D Services, LLC

In short: Your vehicles and service records stay on your device. A few lookups you start are sent directly to public vehicle-data services, as described below. JL3D does not collect, receive or sell any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

  • Vehicles (make, model, year, VIN and nickname), service records, costs, mileage and reminders, in a database on your device.
  • Cached recall and complaint results, so they are available offline.
  • App preferences.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device. iOS may include it in your own iCloud or computer backup; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What leaves your device

NHTSA vPIC (U.S. Department of Transportation)
When you decode a VIN, the VIN is sent to vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov to look up the vehicle's details.
NHTSA recalls and complaints API
To check recalls and complaints, the vehicle's make, model and year are sent to api.nhtsa.gov. No VIN or personal details are sent for this.
Wikipedia
To show a picture of your vehicle model, the make and model are used to look up a public image on en.wikipedia.org.
Carfax and AutoCheck (only if you tap)
The vehicle-history links open those websites in your browser with the VIN. Nothing is sent unless you tap a link, and those sites' own privacy policies apply.

JL3D runs no servers for this app and receives none of this data.

Permissions

Notifications
Used only for the service reminders you set. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in with JL3D.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no use of the advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page is updated before the new version is released, and the effective date above changes.

Questions about privacy? Email JL3D@proton.me. We reply within two business days.
