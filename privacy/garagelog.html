Garage Log Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · JL3D Services, LLC

In short: Your vehicles, service records, photos, reminders and backups stay on your device. To decode a VIN, load model lists and check recalls, the app sends only the vehicle details described below to the U.S. National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA). JL3D runs no servers for this app and does not receive, sell or share your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app stores on your device

  • Vehicles you add (year, make, model, trim, engine, body class, fuel type, VIN if you enter one, colour, licence plate, nickname, odometer readings and purchase details).
  • Service records, parts, costs, shop names, notes and service reminders.
  • Vehicle photos you choose with the system photo picker. The app sees only the photo you pick, saves a resized copy and removes embedded metadata such as location. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Cached NHTSA results (model lists, recalls and complaints), so lookups are not repeated and saved safety results stay available offline.
  • App preferences, such as distance units and whether reminders are on. Reminders and odometer check-ins are local notifications scheduled by iOS on the device.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device. iOS may include it in your own iCloud or computer backup; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What leaves your device

NHTSA vPIC: VIN decoding (Add Vehicle)
When you add a vehicle by VIN and tap to decode it, the 17-character VIN is sent to vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov. Not stored by the app.
NHTSA vPIC: Refresh from VIN (Edit Vehicle)
Only when you tap Refresh from VIN, the vehicle's VIN is sent to vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov. Proposed changes are shown first and nothing is saved until you confirm.
NHTSA vPIC: model lists
When you enter a vehicle by hand, the make and model year you pick are sent to vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov to list models. Lists are cached on the device for 30 days. Make and trim lists are built into the app.
NHTSA recalls and complaints (automatic)
When you open a vehicle and no fresh results are saved, its make, model and model year (never the VIN) are sent to api.nhtsa.gov. Results are cached for 7 days; if a later refresh fails, the saved results stay visible with a Last updated date.
NHTSA.gov: Check open recalls for this VIN (only if you tap)
On a vehicle saved with a VIN, this button opens NHTSA's public recall lookup website (www.nhtsa.gov) in your browser with that VIN, so it can show recalls not yet repaired on that car, as reported by the manufacturer. The VIN is also copied to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you tap it.
Files you choose to create
Back Up Garage makes one .garagelogbackup file with all your vehicles, records, reminders and photos; Export Service History makes a CSV spreadsheet. You choose where they go (for example Files, iCloud Drive or AirDrop). They are never sent to JL3D and are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere private.

Each request uses HTTPS and, like any internet request, reveals your IP address to NHTSA, which handles it under its own privacy policy (nhtsa.gov/about-nhtsa/privacy-policy). Links to NHTSA recall campaign pages carry only the campaign number. Your service records, costs, notes, reminders, licence plate, odometer, photos and backups are never sent to anyone.

JL3D runs no servers for this app and receives none of this data.

App Store privacy label

On the App Store privacy label, the NHTSA lookups are declared conservatively as Other Data, used only for App Functionality, not linked to you and not used for tracking.

Permissions

Notifications
Used only for the service reminders and odometer check-ins you set. They are scheduled on your device.
Photos
No library permission is requested. You pick a single photo with the system photo picker.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in with JL3D.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no use of the advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page is updated before the new version is released, and the effective date above changes.
