FDJ Song Finder Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · JL3D Services, LLC

In short: Your church's song library is downloaded directly from Planning Center using your church access key and stays on your device. JL3D runs no servers for this app and does not collect, receive, or sell any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

  • A local copy of your church's Planning Center Services songs, arrangements, chord charts, lyrics, keys, tempos, and scheduling metadata in a private SQLite database on your device so search and chart reading work offline.
  • Your saved lists (Favorites and Shortlist), recent searches (up to the 10 most recent per library on Android), and display preferences such as Light/Dark appearance and chart text size.
  • Your read-only Planning Center church access key (Application ID and Secret): stored in the iOS Keychain on iPhone and iPad, and encrypted on Android with an AES-256-GCM key held in the Android Keystore inside app-private no-backup storage.
  • On Android, your Planning Center organization name and ID plus a randomly salted SHA-256 hash of the access key's Planning Center person ID, stored locally in the app's private database to prevent mixing libraries from different organizations or accounts.
  • On Android, the downloaded song library, saved lists, recent searches, preferences, and encrypted access key are excluded from Android cloud backup (Google Drive Auto Backup), ADB backup, and device-to-device transfer. On iOS, app storage may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup under Apple's privacy policy.

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device. On Android, this data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. On iPhone and iPad, iOS may include it in your own iCloud or computer backup; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What leaves your device

Planning Center (Ministry Centered Technologies)
The app talks directly and only to Planning Center's HTTPS API (api.planningcenteronline.com) using the read-only church access key you enter, to verify the key and download or refresh your church's Services songs, arrangements, chord charts, and keys. The app sends GET requests only and never edits, creates, or deletes anything in Planning Center. On Android, the app rejects non-HTTPS traffic, redirects, and user-installed certificates. Planning Center's own privacy policy applies to your church's account there. JL3D runs no servers for this app and receives none of this data.
Optional links you tap
If you tap a Planning Center menu link (such as Open in Planning Center, Open Planning Center Songs, or Open Web Music Stand), your device's browser opens that Planning Center web page (services.planningcenteronline.com or musicstand.planningcenteronline.com). The browser handles that visit under its own and Planning Center's privacy policies.
Sharing and copying song titles
On Android, tapping Share on a song sends only the plain song title to the app you choose in the Android share sheet; sharing a Saved list sends only the song titles, one per line. Copying a song title on iOS or Android puts only the plain song title on your clipboard. Lyrics, chord charts, Planning Center IDs, links, and access keys are never shared or copied by those actions.

Planning Center's privacy policy: planningcenter.com/privacy.

JL3D runs no servers for this app and receives none of this data.

Permissions

Network access
Used only to connect over HTTPS to Planning Center (api.planningcenteronline.com) to check your access key and download or refresh your church's song library. No other network permissions or tracking SDKs are included.
Background refresh (iOS)
Used to keep your downloaded library up to date while the app is closed, including an occasional overnight verification check while your iPhone or iPad is charging. You can turn it off in iOS Settings > General > Background App Refresh.
Background refresh (Android)
While signed in, the app schedules WorkManager periodic checks (a refresh every 6 hours when connected, plus an overnight verification sweep around 1:00 AM when on unmetered Wi-Fi and charging). The app does not use foreground services. You can stop background refreshes by restricting background battery or background data for FDJ Song Finder in Android Settings > Apps > FDJ Song Finder, or by signing out in the app.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in with JL3D.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no use of the advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page is updated before the new version is released, and the effective date above changes.
