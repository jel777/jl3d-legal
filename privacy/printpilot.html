Print Pilot Privacy Policy

Effective 16 October 2026 · JL3D Services, LLC

In short: The documents and photos you work with stay on your device. JL3D does not collect, receive or sell any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

  • Documents, PDFs and photos you import, the layouts you build from them, and their thumbnails.
  • Print settings, presets and your local print history.
  • App preferences. If you updated from an older version, a copy of any data that could not be migrated is kept in the app's Documents folder under "Previous Version Export".

This data lives in the app's private storage on your device. iOS may include it in your own iCloud or computer backup; that is governed by Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What leaves your device

Your printer
When you print, the page you chose is sent directly from your device to the AirPrint printer you select on your local network. It does not pass through JL3D.
Apple App Store (subscriptions)
Print Pilot Pro is an auto-renewable subscription sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; JL3D receives no payment card, name or contact details.

JL3D runs no servers for this app and receives none of this data.

Permissions

Photos
Only the photos you pick are imported. The app does not browse your library.
Camera (Pro document scanner)
Used only while you scan a document. Scanning happens on the device.
Local network
Used to find and print to AirPrint printers on your Wi-Fi network.

Purchases

Core printing and PDF export are free. Print Pilot Pro is available as a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription through Apple. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless it is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the period. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple Account settings.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in with JL3D.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and no use of the advertising identifier.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page is updated before the new version is released, and the effective date above changes.

Questions about privacy? Email JL3D@proton.me. We reply within two business days.
